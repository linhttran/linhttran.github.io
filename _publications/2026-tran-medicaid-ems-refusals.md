Transport refusals after nonfatal overdoses may increase risk of adverse events. Yet, concerns over costs of emergency care may deter patients from receiving appropriate care. Using 2018-2024 data from the National Emergency Medical Services Information, we examine whether increased insurance coverage through the Medicaid expansion affected rates of transport refusal rates for nonfatal overdoses involving emergency medical services (“EMS”). We use staggered difference-in-differences to compare trends in refusals between states that expanded Medicaid during the study period versus states that have yet to expand Medicaid. Preliminary evidence suggests that Medicaid expansion is associated with a reduction in transport refusal rates.
