We examine the impact of openings of large for-profit psychiatric hospitals on access and quality of care. Inpatient psychiatric care is particularly important for patients with serious mental illness. The rise of for-profit psychiatric hospitals may increase supply of specialized inpatient psychiatric care, thereby addressing unmet need. However, patients may be harmed if these new facilities are lower in quality. We leverage administrative claims data and a difference-in-differences approach to examine the effects of new psychiatric facilities and new for-profit psychiatric facilities on access to inpatient psychiatric care and patient outcomes.
